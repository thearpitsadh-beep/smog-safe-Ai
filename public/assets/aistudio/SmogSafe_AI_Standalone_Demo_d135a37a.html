HEALTHIER HABITS AT HOME

Make indoors matter.

Useful, evidence-aware ways to limit indoor particle sources and make your space more comfortable.

START WITH THE BASICS

Cleaner air isn't always about buying more.

Reducing smoke sources and controlling dust are often sensible first steps. Ventilation and filtration choices depend on your indoor and outdoor conditions.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION

Plants are lovely—not a HEPA substitute.

Houseplants should not be presented as a proven way to clear dangerous levels of particulate pollution indoors. Choose effective source control and appropriate filtration when needed.

THE WINDOW QUESTION

Opening windows is a trade-off.

Outdoor smog can enter through open windows. But poorly ventilated rooms can also trap indoor-generated pollutants. Consider local conditions, cooking activities and official guidance.
